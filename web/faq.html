Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. About Dream247

What is Dream247?
Dream247 is a token-based e-commerce shopping platform designed to make shopping more rewarding and engaging. Users add money to their Dream247 Shopping Wallet and receive equivalent Shopping Credits (Tokens) which can be used to shop for products and participate in loyalty-based activities on the platform.
How is Dream247 different from other shopping apps?
Unlike traditional discount-based platforms, Dream247 uses a token and loyalty system. Users receive Shopping Credits equal to the amount they add, with no expiry, encouraging long-term engagement and smarter shopping.

2. Shopping Credits (Tokens)

What are Dream247 Shopping Credits / Tokens?
Shopping Credits (also called Tokens) are digital credits issued by Dream247. These credits are used exclusively within the Dream247 platform for shopping and loyalty benefits.
How do I get Shopping Credits?
When you add money to your Dream247 Shopping Wallet:
  • ₹100 added = 100 Shopping Credits
  • Credits are issued at a 1:1 value
  • Credits have no expiry
Do Shopping Credits expire?
No. Dream247 Shopping Credits never expire and remain valid as long as your account is active.
Can I convert Shopping Credits back to cash?
No. Shopping Credits are non-withdrawable and cannot be converted into cash or transferred to a bank account.

3. Usage of Shopping Credits

Where can I use Shopping Credits?
Shopping Credits can be used only on the Dream247 platform for:
  • Purchasing products
  • Loyalty-based benefits
  • Platform-specific offers and engagement activities
Can Shopping Credits be transferred to another user?
No. Shopping Credits are non-transferable and can only be used by the account holder.

4. Wallet & Payments

What is the Dream247 Shopping Wallet?
The Dream247 Shopping Wallet is a closed-loop wallet where users add money and receive equivalent Shopping Credits for use on the platform.
Is my wallet balance refundable?
No. Once money is added to the wallet and converted into Shopping Credits, it becomes non-refundable, except in rare and exceptional cases (see Refund Policy below).

5. Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

Can I cancel an order?
Order cancellation is not guaranteed once an order is placed. Cancellation may still not be allowed if the order has not been processed or shipped, subject to Dream247's discretion.
Does Dream247 offer returns?
No. Dream247 follows a No Return Policy. All purchases are considered final unless explicitly mentioned otherwise.
Does Dream247 provide refunds?
Dream247 follows a No Refund Policy.
Refunds are provided only in rare and exceptional cases, and strictly at the discretion of the Dream247 team, after:
  • Internal verification
  • Order review
  • Compliance and fraud checks
The decision made by Dream247 in such cases is final and binding.
In what cases can a refund be considered?
Refunds may be considered only in exceptional scenarios such as:
  • Duplicate payment due to technical failure
  • Proven transaction errors verified by Dream247
Refunds are not guaranteed and may be denied even after review.

6. Loyalty & Engagement System

How does the loyalty system work?
Dream247 uses Shopping Credits as a loyalty mechanism. Users are rewarded for shopping and engaging with the platform, encouraging long-term usage without expiration pressure.
Is Dream247 a money gaming or gambling app?
No. Dream247 is a shopping and loyalty engagement platform. It does not offer money withdrawal, real-money gaming, or gambling services.

7. Account & Compliance

What happens if my account is inactive?
If an account remains inactive for an extended period, Dream247 reserves the right to suspend or close the account as per platform policies. Any remaining Shopping Credits will be handled according to applicable terms.
Can Dream247 change these policies?
Yes. Dream247 reserves the right to update or modify its policies, FAQs, and terms at any time. Changes will be communicated through the app or website.

8. Support & Contact

How can I contact Dream247 support?
You can reach our support team through:
  • In-app support
  • Official email or contact page on the Dream247 website
